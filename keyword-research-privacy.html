Keyword Research privacy policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

This policy covers CloseAvatar Keyword Research, an internal tool described on the Keyword Research page. The rest of closeavatar.com is covered by our main privacy policy.

Who signs in

Only CloseAvatar's founder signs in, with Google, using CloseAvatar's own Google account. The tool has no other users and collects no information about members of the public.

What Google data the tool accesses

With the owner's permission (Google OAuth, scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/adwords), the tool calls the Google Ads API to request keyword ideas and their search-volume, competition and bid estimates. It does not read campaign, billing or customer data, and it makes no changes to the Google Ads account.

Storage

The sign-in token is stored only on the owner's own computer, in a file readable only by that user. Keyword results are saved as research notes on the same computer. Nothing is stored on CloseAvatar's servers.

Sharing

We do not sell, share or transfer Google user data to anyone, and we do not use it for advertising. CloseAvatar's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access and deletion

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking access and deleting the local token file removes the tool's access completely. For questions or deletion requests, contact us.
