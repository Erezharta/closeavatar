CloseAvatar Keyword Research

Last updated: October 25, 2026

CloseAvatar Keyword Research is an internal tool that CloseAvatar uses for its own market research. It is not a public product, and nobody outside CloseAvatar signs in to it.

What it does

The tool uses the Google Ads API (Keyword Planner) to look up search demand for topics we are researching: average monthly searches, competition and estimated bid ranges for a list of keywords in a given country and language. We use this to decide which products and markets are worth building for.

What it does not do

It only reads keyword-planning data. It does not create, change or pause campaigns, ads, budgets or billing, and it does not manage Google Ads accounts for anyone else.

Who uses it

Only CloseAvatar's founder, signed in with CloseAvatar's own Google Ads manager account.

Data handling is described in the Keyword Research privacy policy.
