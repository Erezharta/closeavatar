The problem with sales practice

Reps learn on live deals because nothing else pushes back.

01

Roleplay is polite

A teammate playing the buyer goes easy, breaks character, and has never been the person who signs the contract.

02

Feedback arrives after the loss

Call reviews happen once the deal is already gone. By then the habit is set and the moment is a blur.

03

Training is generic, gaps aren’t

One rep skips the next-step ask. Another folds on price. A single workshop can’t fix both.

01 — THE BUYER

A buyer with a past, a mood and something to hide.

Every avatar has a role, priorities and a history with vendors like you. Some are friendly. Some are tired of being sold to. Most are holding back the one thing that decides the deal.

They stay in character for the whole call and react to what you actually say, not to a script.

02 — THE COACHING

Feedback that points at the exact line.

Not “work on objections.” CloseAvatar marks the sentence that lost the buyer, says why it landed badly, and gives you a better line to try.

Discovery, objection handling and closing are scored separately, so you know what to fix first.
