Features

Reps get reps. Managers get visibility.

Most sales training is a slide deck and a roleplay with a colleague who's being polite. CloseAvatar gives every rep a realistic buyer to practice against, any time.

Scenario library

Ready-made situations for the moments that decide deals, so reps practice the hard conversations on purpose.

  • Discovery calls
  • Objection handling
  • Pricing and negotiation
  • Closing and next steps

Buyer avatars

Each avatar has a role, priorities, and attitude. They push back, go quiet, or change the subject, and they stay in character the whole call.

  • Different roles and seniority
  • Friendly to skeptical
  • Responds to what you actually say

Team coaching

Give managers a clear view of where the team is strong and where it stalls, and turn that into focused practice instead of generic training.

  • Feedback on every session
  • Patterns across a team
  • Targeted follow-up drills
How we use Claude

CloseAvatar is built on the Claude API.

We're a product, not an agency. Claude does three specific jobs inside CloseAvatar.

Multi-turn buyer-avatar dialogue, in character

Claude plays the buyer across a full conversation. It holds the persona, remembers what was said earlier, and reacts to your actual answers, so the call feels like a real one rather than a script.

Scoring discovery, objection handling and closing

After the call, Claude reviews the transcript and scores how you handled discovery, objections, and the close, pointing to the specific moments behind each score.

Next drill, generated from your weak moments

Claude turns the moments you struggled with into the next personalized drill, so the following session targets your gaps instead of repeating what you already do well.

Practice call→Scored feedback→Personalized drill→Practice again

AI scores are coaching input, not a verdict on a rep. We're early, and we're shaping the product with the teams who try it.
