Privacy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

This page covers the CloseAvatar marketing website at closeavatar.com. CloseAvatar is an early-stage product, and this policy will be expanded before the product itself is made generally available.

What this website collects

This site is static. It has no accounts, forms, advertising, or analytics scripts, and it sets no cookies of its own. Like any web host, the hosting provider may keep standard server logs (such as IP address and requested page) for security and operations.

Email

If you email erez@closeavatar.com, for example to request early access, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to write. We use it only to reply to you and to run the early-access program. We don't sell it.

The CloseAvatar product

CloseAvatar uses the Claude API from Anthropic to power buyer-avatar conversations and coaching. Early-access participants will receive details on how practice-session data is handled before they start.

Your requests

To ask what we hold about you, or to have it deleted, contact erez@closeavatar.com.
